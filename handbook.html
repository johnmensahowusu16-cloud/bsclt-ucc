About the Programme

BSc Laboratory Technology — Overview

The BSc Laboratory Technology programme at the University of Cape Coast trains competent laboratory scientists for careers in medical, industrial, research, and educational laboratories. Students gain hands-on skills in analytical chemistry, microbiology, haematology, clinical chemistry, instrumentation, and advanced diagnostic techniques.

The four-year programme combines classroom learning with practical laboratory sessions, industrial attachment, and a final-year research project to prepare graduates for professional practice.

Programme Duration

4 years (8 semesters) • Full-time • BSc Degree
