About

BSc Laboratory Technology The Department of Laboratory Technology at the University of Cape Coast trains competent laboratory scientists for medical, industrial, research, and educational laboratories. Our four-year programme combines coursework, hands-on practice, industrial attachment, and a final-year research project.
