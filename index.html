About the Department

BSc Laboratory Technology The Department of Laboratory Technology at the University of Cape Coast trains competent laboratory scientists for careers in medical, industrial, research, and educational laboratories. Our four-year programme combines rigorous coursework, hands-on laboratory practice, industrial attachment, and a final-year research project.
